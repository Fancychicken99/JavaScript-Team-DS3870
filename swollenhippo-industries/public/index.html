Our mission

Help US domestic manufacturers turn hardware concepts into functional prototypes and move confidently toward production. We bring physical parts, electronics, firmware, and industrial design together so teams can test ideas, refine designs, and prepare for manufacturing through a coordinated engineering workflow.

Capabilities & services

Multidisciplinary prototyping support for US domestic manufacturing teams.

Additive & subtractive manufacturing

FDM, SLA, and SLS 3D printing paired with multi-axis CNC milling for precise physical parts and functional assemblies.

Electronics & PCB design

Schematic capture, printed circuit board (PCB) layout, component sourcing, rapid assembly, and embedded hardware validation testing.

Software & firmware development

Microcontroller firmware in C and C++, hardware-to-cloud Internet of Things (IoT) connections, diagnostic interfaces, and control software.

Digital & industrial design

Design for manufacturability, ergonomic enclosure styling, computer-aided design (CAD) modeling, and user interaction design.

Why partner with SwollenHippo?

Domestic focus

Prototyping and engineering support focused on domestic manufacturing teams, with close collaboration throughout development.

Unified engineering workflow

Coordinate enclosures, circuits, and code together, with a single point of contact across disciplines.

Rapid iteration

Move from initial CAD models toward a functional proof of concept through practical build, test, and refinement cycles.
